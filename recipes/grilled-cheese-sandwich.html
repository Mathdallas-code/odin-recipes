Grilled Cheese Sandwich

Description

The grilled cheese sandwich is an all-time classic food. It is a basic sandwich prepared using white bread, butter/mayonnaise, and cheese. The cheese can be of any type, but cheddar is mostly used. It is then placed on a ( non-stick ) pan and grilled until one side of the bread is golden brown, before flipping it to grill the other side. The sandwich is then served, with any extra choices of ketchup, mayonnaise, or mustard.

Ingredients ( for 2 servings )

  • 4 slices of white bread
  • 3 tablespoons butter, divided
  • 2 slices of cheese

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Preheat a nonstick skillet over medium heat. Generously butter one side each bread slice.
  3. Place 1 slice bread, butter-side down, in the hot skillet; top with 1 slice cheese.
  4. Place 1 slice bread, butter-side up, on top cheese.
  5. Cook until lightly browned on one side; flip and continue cooking until cheese is melted.
  6. Repeat with remaining bread and cheese slices. Serve and enjoy!
